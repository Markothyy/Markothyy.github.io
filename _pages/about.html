About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Vanderbilt University, finishing a B.S. with a minor in digital fabrication in May 2027.

The part of engineering I enjoy most is design: taking an idea through CAD, drawings, and fabrication, then testing it to see what holds up. At the Vanderbilt Aerospace Design Lab I work on test equipment, flight hardware, and tooling for the team's NASA Student Launch rocket. Last summer I was a tunnel engineering intern at The Boring Company, where I designed tooling and storage for two tunnel boring machines. For senior design, I'm part of a four-person team building an in-space manufacturing payload.

I'm looking for design, test, systems, and build engineering roles in aerospace and defense after I graduate.
