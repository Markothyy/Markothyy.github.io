About

Mark Sedek is a mechanical engineering student at Vanderbilt University with a minor in digital fabrication, expected to graduate in May 2027.

As a payload engineer at the Vanderbilt Aerospace Design Laboratory, he designs test equipment, flight hardware, and tooling for the NASA Student Launch team, including two scaled vibration test benches and the vehicle avionics bays. In summer 2026, as a tunnel engineering intern at The Boring Company, he owned the tooling subsystem for two tunnel boring machines. His senior design team is developing a semi-autonomous in-space manufacturing payload for the COSMIC Capstone Challenge.

Seeking full-time mechanical engineering roles in design, test, and manufacturing for aerospace and defense.
