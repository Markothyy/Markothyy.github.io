## Role

Design Engineer, August 2025 to present. Test equipment, flight hardware, and vehicle tooling.

## Work

- **[Vibration test benches](/portfolio/vibration-test-benches/):** two scaled benches, built from off-the-shelf components, for vibration testing PCBs, connections, and assemblies before flight. Component bench 0–4 kHz; assembly bench 0–63 Hz, up to 1.5 lb.
- **[Avionics bays](/portfolio/avionics-bay/):** subscale and full-scale bays with a 3D-printed PETG sled for altimeters, batteries, and rotary switches, mounted to CNC-machined bulkheads with threaded rods.
- **[Airframe cutting jig](/portfolio/Carbon%20Fiber%20Cutting%20Jig/):** holds ±1/32 in perpendicularity and flatness on carbon fiber airframe and coupler sections from 4 in to 4 ft long.
- **Payload gimbal concepts:** camera and laser housing concepts for a 2-DOF gimbal, covering lightweighting, shock isolation, and damping pad selection.

## Documentation

- Coauthor, design sections of the team's NASA Preliminary Design Review
- Analysis of wind loads that could tip the deployed payload (in progress)
