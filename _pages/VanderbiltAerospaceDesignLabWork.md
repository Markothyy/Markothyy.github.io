## What I work on

I joined the lab in August 2025. My work so far has been on test equipment, flight hardware, and the tooling the team uses to build the vehicle.

- **[Vibration test benches](/portfolio/vibration-test-benches/):** two scaled benches for shaking PCBs, connectors, and assemblies before they fly. This is my main focus this year.
- **[Avionics bays](/portfolio/avionics-bay/):** bays for the subscale and full-scale vehicles that hold the altimeters, batteries, and arming switches.
- **[Airframe cutting jig](/portfolio/Carbon%20Fiber%20Cutting%20Jig/):** a jig for square, repeatable cuts on carbon fiber airframe sections.
- **Payload gimbal concepts:** early camera and laser housing concepts for the payload's 2-DOF gimbal, focused on lightweighting and shock isolation, including selecting a damping pad to protect the optics.

## Documentation

NASA reviews each team's design at several milestones. I coauthored design sections of the team's Preliminary Design Review and am writing the analysis of wind loads that could tip the deployed payload.
